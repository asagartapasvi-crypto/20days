7. Types of Ownership Pattern

📄 Sem3_BNM-021 JOURNALISM AND MEDIA IN THE DIGITAL AGE.pdf
Ownership patterns: individual ownership, corporation ownership, group/chain ownership, horizontal integration, vertical integration and cross-media ownership.

Direct and strong coverage. This is the principal source for ownership patterns.

8. Individual Ownership

📄 Sem3_BNM-021 JOURNALISM AND MEDIA IN THE DIGITAL AGE.pdf
Individual ownership: ownership and control by an individual; discussion of its place among media ownership forms.

Study as the first ownership pattern and compare its control structure with corporate and group ownership.

9. Corporation Ownership

📄 Sem3_BNM-021 JOURNALISM AND MEDIA IN THE DIGITAL AGE.pdf
Corporation ownership: ownership through a legally constituted corporation; financial capacity, expansion and management implications.

Prepare definition, characteristics, advantages and limitations.

10. Group / Chain Ownership

📄 Sem3_BNM-021 JOURNALISM AND MEDIA IN THE DIGITAL AGE.pdf
Group/chain ownership: one organisation controlling two or more media outlets; centralised financial, administrative and human-resource management; economies of scale.

Prepare meaning, features, advantages and possible concentration-of-control issues.

11. Horizontal Integration

📄 Sem3_BNM-021 JOURNALISM AND MEDIA IN THE DIGITAL AGE.pdf
Horizontal integration: ownership/operation across different media segments; wider visibility and revenue opportunities.

Study meaning, features, advantages and its effect on media reach.

12. Vertical Integration

📄 Sem3_BNM-021 JOURNALISM AND MEDIA IN THE DIGITAL AGE.pdf
Vertical integration: control over different stages involved in production and distribution; greater control over the media production chain.

Prepare meaning, features, advantages and possible limitations.

13. Cross-Media Ownership

📄 Sem3_BNM-021 JOURNALISM AND MEDIA IN THE DIGITAL AGE.pdf
Cross-media ownership: ownership of different types of media organisations such as newspapers, magazines and television; wider reach and possible cost advantages.

Prepare definition, examples from the source and advantages/disadvantages.

14. Advantages and Disadvantages of Ownership Patterns

📄 Sem3_BNM-021 JOURNALISM AND MEDIA IN THE DIGITAL AGE.pdf
The ownership section discusses financial strength, economies of scale, wider reach, centralised management, resource sharing and the possible problems associated with concentration/control.

Prepare a comparison table of the major ownership patterns with advantages and disadvantages.

Recommended reading order: Organisational Structure of Newspaper → Functions → Editorial Department → Circulation → Advertising → Production → Ownership Patterns → Advantages and Disadvantages.
