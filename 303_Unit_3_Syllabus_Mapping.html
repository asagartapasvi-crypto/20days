📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_SYBA-JOURNALISM III.pdf

Very strong direct coverage. This is the main reading section for the recommendations.

4. Press Council of India

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_SYBA-JOURNALISM III.pdf
The material explains the Press Council as a self-regulatory mechanism intended to deal with violations of journalistic ethics, maintain high standards, safeguard press freedom and act as a watchdog and defender of press freedom. It states that under the Press Council Act, 1978, the first Press Council was constituted in 1979.

Strong direct coverage. Use this section for the meaning, purpose and broad role of the Press Council.

8. Press Freedom and Self-Regulation

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_SYBA-JOURNALISM III.pdf
The material presents self-regulation as preferable to direct government control because it can protect freedom of thought and expression. The Press Council is described as a mechanism that combines dealing with ethical violations with defending press freedom.

Direct supporting concept. Useful for explaining why the Press Council is important in a democratic press system.

9. Historical Development: Press Council and Regulation

📄 Sem3_PRINT MEDIA IN IND.pdf
The material states that the First Press Commission was constituted in 1952 to help newspapers maintain independence, raise standards through a code of conduct and maintain professional and public-taste standards. It states that the Press Council Act, 1965 was passed on the basis of its recommendations and the Press Council of India was established in 1966.

Useful historical background. Compare this with the supplied SYBA material's discussion of the 1978 Act and 1979 reconstitution, without silently reconciling differences in the source accounts.

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_SYBA-JOURNALISM III.pdf
The material states that the Second Press Commission was appointed under Kuldip Nayar to examine all aspects of the press. Related historical coverage. Prepare as a short note if the examination asks about press commissions beyond the First Commission.
