8. Leads

302 Sem3 Unit 4 Editing.pdf

The Headline and Lead Writing material defines the lead as the opening paragraph of a news story, discusses its role in attracting attention, and covers the 5Ws and 1H and Subject-Verb-Object approaches. It also presents different kinds of leads, including You Lead, Blind Lead, Staccato Lead, Contrast Lead, Question Lead, Caption Lead, Summary Lead and Descriptive Lead.

Direct and very strong match. Study the types, purpose and construction of leads.

9. Principles of Rewriting

302 Sem3 Unit 4 Editing.pdf

The Editing material defines rewriting as writing again and verifying facts, figures and formats. It explains that rewriting is needed when the original story is unclear or poorly structured, and gives a process involving organisation of facts, paragraph construction, use of quotes and factual analysis.

Direct and very strong match. Main source: Rewriting News Stories.
