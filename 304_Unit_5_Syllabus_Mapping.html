2. Techniques of Pre-test and Post-test

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_RMDC Block-2.pdf
The research material explicitly explains experimental design as a process involving pre-test, experiment and post-test. It also covers the pre-test-post-test control-group design, post-test-only control group and Solomon four-group design, including the purpose of comparison and control.

Direct and strong coverage for the general pre-test/post-test research concept. It is a supporting research-method source rather than a dedicated advertising pre-test chapter.

3. Pre-test in Advertising

📄 Sem3 304 ADVERTISING.pdf
The advertising source discusses creating advertising messages, media selection and assessing effectiveness, which provide the advertising context for testing campaign elements.

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_RMDC Block-2.pdf
The research source explains how pre-testing fits into experimental research and how pre-measurement can be used as a basis for comparison.

Related/partial advertising-specific coverage, but strong methodological support. The supplied advertising source does not provide a detailed standalone list of advertising pre-test techniques.

4. Post-test in Advertising

📄 Sem3 304 ADVERTISING.pdf
The advertising source directly discusses assessing advertising effectiveness after implementation and explains that evaluation provides direction for future campaigns.

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_RMDC Block-2.pdf
The research source explains post-test designs and comparison between groups after exposure to an experimental stimulus.

Strong related coverage. Use the advertising source for campaign evaluation and the research source for methodological understanding of post-testing.

5. Process of Market Research

📄 Sem3 304 ADVERTISING.pdf
The advertising material links advertising planning with research, customer knowledge, media consumption, audience profile, campaign goals, reach and coverage.

📄 301 Sem3 MedSocTheories_Tech determin_PublicSphere_Audience_Feminist.pdf
The audience-research material explains research of specific audience segments to gather, analyse and interpret information about attitudes, knowledge, interests, behaviour and preferences. It notes the use of surveys, online forums, interviews and focus groups.

Strong related coverage. Together these sources establish the purpose, audience orientation and practical use of market/audience research in advertising.

6. Research Methods in Market Research

📄 Sem3_MASS COMMUNICATION RESEARCH.pdf
The research material covers research methods, data collection, interviews and data analysis. It explains interviews as a direct verbal method of collecting data and discusses analysis and presentation of collected data.

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_RMDC Block-5.pdf
The research-methods material explains observation, interviewing, focus group discussion and case study, including structured/semi-structured interviews and the stages of planning, selecting, moderating and analysing a focus group.

📄 301 Sem3 MedSocTheories_Tech determin_PublicSphere_Audience_Feminist.pdf
Audience research is described through surveys/questionnaires, market research online forums, interviews and focus groups.

Strong supporting coverage. These sources provide the actual research-method foundation required for Unit 5.

7. Survey / Questionnaire Method

📄 301 Sem3 MedSocTheories_Tech determin_PublicSphere_Audience_Feminist.pdf
The audience-research material identifies surveys with questionnaires as a research format and explains their use in understanding audience characteristics, preferences and media behaviour.

Direct related coverage. Useful for market/audience research preparation.

8. Interviews

📄 Sem3_MASS COMMUNICATION RESEARCH.pdf
The source describes interviews as a systematic, direct method of collecting data through verbal interaction. It identifies personal and telephonic interviews.

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_RMDC Block-5.pdf
The source identifies structured, unstructured and semi-structured interviews and discusses good interviewing practice.

Direct supporting coverage. Prepare definition, types and basic use in market research.

9. Focus Group Discussion

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_RMDC Block-5.pdf
Focus group discussion is described as a qualitative research method/group interview. Its main phases are planning, selection of the group, moderating the discussion, and analysing/reporting.

📄 301 Sem3 MedSocTheories_Tech determin_PublicSphere_Audience_Feminist.pdf
Focus groups are listed as one of the formats used in audience research.

Direct supporting coverage. Particularly useful for qualitative market/audience research.

10. Observation

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_RMDC Block-5.pdf
Observation is identified as a research method and its limitation of observer bias is noted.

Direct supporting research-method coverage, though not presented specifically as an advertising-market-research chapter.

11. Qualitative and Quantitative Research

📄 Sem3_EVENT MANAGEMENT.pdf
The marketing-research section distinguishes quantitative research, which focuses on numerical/objective data and can use surveys and content analysis, from qualitative research, which focuses on subjective patterns/themes and can use focus groups, field observation and in-depth interviews.

Strong supporting coverage for classifying market-research methods. This is useful for organising the research-methods part of Unit 5.

12. Audience Research as Market-Research Support

📄 301 Sem3 MedSocTheories_Tech determin_PublicSphere_Audience_Feminist.pdf
Audience research gathers and interprets information on attitudes, knowledge, interests, behaviour and preferences. Advertisers use it to understand audience needs, reactions to products, motivation and persuasion. Demographic categories and target-audience profiling are also discussed.

Very strong supporting topic. It connects market research directly with advertising decisions.

13. Data Analysis and Interpretation

📄 Sem3_MASS COMMUNICATION RESEARCH.pdf
The research source explains rigorous analysis of collected data, use of appropriate tests according to research methods and hypotheses, and interpretation/generalisation of findings.

📄 301 Sem3 Devlp Jurnl_RMDC Block-5.pdf
The research-methods source describes analysing and reporting information as part of methods such as focus group discussion and case study.

Supporting coverage. The syllabus only says research methods, but data analysis is an important part of completing the research process.

14. Research Limitations / Quality of Audience Data

📄 301 Sem3 MedSocTheories_Tech determin_PublicSphere_Audience_Feminist.pdf
The audience-research source notes that research can be time-consuming and costly, questions may be biased, and vague or incomplete responses can make results invalid.

Direct supporting point for evaluating the quality and limitations of market/audience research.
